# Edo.ai – AI Ad Creator & Video Studio

> **Edo.ai** is a modern, AI-powered ad creation studio designed for digital marketers, business owners, and agencies. It enables users to instantly generate high-converting ad copies, HD image creatives, dynamic video ad concepts, platform-specific targeting keywords, and optimized hashtags across all major social media and advertising networks.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🔐 Multi-Method Authentication:** Secure sign-in supporting Google OAuth, Email/Password, and Mobile Phone Number verification with an automated guest paywall.
* **🎯 Dynamic Platform-Specific Keyword Engine:** Tailored targeting keywords automatically generated based on the selected ad platform (Meta / Facebook & Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Google Ads, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and X).
* **🖼️ Product Image Upload & Live Preview:** Drag-and-drop your product image to preview live ad mockups inside a mobile device frame.
* **#️⃣ Platform-Optimized Hashtags:** Automatically generates the maximum optimal hashtags based on the chosen social network's algorithm.
* **💎 Freemium & Tiered Pricing Model:** 
  - **Free Trial:** 15-day trial period, 5 initial free credits, and 1 Image Ad creation per day.
  - **EDO Pro ($19/mo):** Unlimited 4K Video Ads, AI Voiceovers, and advanced Meta Ads interest targeting keywords.
* **📋 One-Click Copy Actions:** Instantly copy headlines, descriptions, target keywords, and hashtags directly into your campaigns.

## 💻 Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 / Tailwind CSS (Responsive Flexbox & Grid layout)
* **Interactivity:** Vanilla JavaScript (Dynamic DOM manipulation, login authentication simulation, credit counting, and one-click clipboard copying)

## 📦 Getting Started

To run or preview this project locally:

1. Clone or download the repository to your local machine.
2. Open the main project file (`index.html` or your designated entry point) in any modern web browser.
3. Sign in using Google, Email, or Phone to test the 5 free initial credits and ad studio dashboard.
